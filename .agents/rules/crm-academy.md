# The chess academy: scope, tuition packages, branches and the bookshop

Read this before changing what an opportunity sells, the tuition packages, the branches, the lead sources, the Lark Base import, or anything that would bring books, stock or students' academic records into the CRM.

**Scope.** This deployment of NocoCRM serves the academy's enrolment only: a parent's enquiry (a lead), the trial lesson, the tuition deal (an opportunity, `pipelineType` `tuition`) and a school's training contract (`b2b_school`). Two neighbouring applications hold the rest, and the CRM does not copy what they keep:

- The bookshop application (NocoBookshop) holds products, stock, orders and its own customers, who need not be parents. An opportunity is never a sale of books or equipment: `pipelineType` no longer offers `merchandise`, and `businessType` no longer offers `chessBook` or `chessEquipment`.
- The academy's operations application (the NocoBase Core application `scripts/nococrm-sync-students.js` writes to) holds students, classes and attendance. The CRM keeps a student only as far as enrolment needs (`crmStudents`, filled when a lead with a student's name is converted, shown on the parent's page) and has no Students entry in its menu, removed on purpose on 2026-11-04.

**A value no longer offered** stays on the records that hold it: `CrmService.save` accepts a select's value outside its options when it is the value the record already had, the form lists that value for that record alone (`choices` in `client/components/crm/form.tsx`), and its label stays in `client/locales/crm/index.ts` and `vi-VN.ts`, saying where it went. So retiring an option needs no data migration; remove it from `server/providers/crm/schema.ts` and keep its label. Retired so far: `merchandise`, `chessBook`, `chessEquipment`, and the package names from before the fee notice, `course12`, `course24`, `course48`.

**Tuition packages** are the fee notice sent to parents, in `server/providers/crm/tuition.ts`, the one place a price changes: 10 sessions 1,500,000 VND with books 200,000; 20 sessions 3,000,000 with books 200,000 and a chess set given (worth 200,000); 40 sessions 5,500,000 (500,000 saved on the per-session price of 150,000), books waived (worth 300,000) and a chess domino set given (worth 100,000); a session is 75 minutes. Each is an opportunity business type (`course10`, `course20`, `course40`); `chessCourse` is any other number of sessions and `course1on1` private coaching. Choosing a package on the opportunity form sets the amount to what the parent pays, sessions and books (1,700,000, 3,200,000, 5,500,000), in VND, which the person can still change, and shows the package's terms under the choice (`packageTerms`, strings `tuition.*`). The file has no imports, so the browser bundle takes it, as `limits.ts`. The CRM records the payment; the books and gifts are the bookshop's stock, which it issues, and nothing in the CRM counts them. A new package is a row there, a business type in `chessBusinessTypes`, and its label in the two locale files; `tests/logic/tuition.test.ts` checks the totals and that every package is offered.

**Branches** are codes, `cs1`, `cs2` and `online` (`preferredBranches`): `cs1` is Kim Liên (22 ngõ 11 Lương Định Của) and `cs2` Vĩnh Phúc (16 phố Vĩnh Phúc, Ngọc Hà). A record never holds a branch's name.

**The Lark Base import** (seed `202611050002_import_lark_crm_data`, data in `database/main/seeds/data/lark_crm_full.json`) wrote Lark's own values at first: the stage `closed_won`, the lead status `Đang chăm sóc`, branches by name and no business type, so the boards, filters and reports missed 349 won tuition deals. Migration `202611190001_crm_academy_values` wrote the CRM's values over them (a deal's package read from its name, `Học phí khóa <n> buổi`), and the seed now writes them itself on a fresh installation. Whatever imports records next writes the CRM's own option values. The imported leads' sources are still Lark's words (`PH giới thiệu`, `FP CLB` and the like), shown as written. `crmProducts`, the 37 books the import brought, is read by nothing in the CRM and is kept until the bookshop application has taken it over; dropping it before then would lose that catalogue, and the seed fills it on a fresh installation, so drop the table and that part of the seed together.

**Leads from the bookshop.** A bookshop customer who asks about lessons becomes a lead with the source `bookshop`. The bookshop application creates it through the CRM's own API, `POST <base path>/api/leads`, with an `x-api-key` header (Settings → API keys) of a user who sells, since a lead's owner must hold a sales role: `name`, `phone`, `source: "bookshop"`, `ownerId`, and what was asked as `message`. The phone number is the shared identity: the CRM normalizes it on save and its duplicate check (`GET /api/duplicateCheck`) finds a parent already known. Nothing flows the other way, and customers are not synchronized.
